8 Sandyford Hall Lawn is a beautifully presented and light filled 4-bedroom semi-detached family home with a private secluded rear garden, located in a quiet cul de sac setting in this most sought-after development of Sandyford Hall, Sandyford, Dublin 18.

The accommodation comprises of an entrance hall, spacious living room with box bay window, dining room, kitchen / breakfast room, utility room and a guest wc. The first floor offers a landing with attic access, a shelved hot press, four bedrooms, (2 doubles with master bedroom ensuite and 2 singles), and a family bathroom.

Outside, the front of the property benefits from off street parking with mature hedging to the side. A gated passage leads one to the private and spacious mature rear garden, which is presented in patio, lawn, mature trees and planting and a timber storage shed to the side. The garden is the perfect setting for children to play and the family to enjoy al fresco entertaining.

Number 8 is within walking distance of local shops at Sandyford Hall shopping centre, and a short drive of Sandyford and Stepaside Villages offering a host of shopping and dining amenities. There are plenty of activities nearby including Fernhill Park & Gardens, Westwood gym, golf clubs, equestrian centres, not to mention an abundance of nearby hill walking and mountain bike routes. Other nearby amenities include Dundrum Town Centre, Carrickmines Retail Outlet, Sandyford Business Park, Beacon South Quarter and Leopardstown Racecourse.

There is an excellent selection of local schools including Stepaside Educate Together, Gaelscoil Thaobh na Coille, St. Mary’s, Holy Trinity, Rosemont School, Our Lady of The Wayside and Kilternan Church of Ireland are all in easy reach. Public transportation is well catered for including the Luas Green Line (Glencairn) and several Bus routes. Dublin City Centre is approximately 25 minutes by car. The M50 and N11 road networks are both just minutes from the home also.

Another benefit of the property is the opportunity to extend to the rear and convert the attic as some neighbours have, subject to the relevant planning permissions. Number 8 will no doubt appeal to a wide range of potential purchaser.

Viewing is highly recommended.
